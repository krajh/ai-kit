# Why "Shade"?

> *"I call to the realm beyond cold and darkness, where death has no meaning for life has no place. Let mine be the dark beyond darkness, falling on the final road to the end of all things. Let mine be the shadow of death."*
>
> — Shade's Summoning Incantation, _He Who Fights With Monsters_

**Shade** is a _Shadow of the Reaper_ — a summoned familiar from [Shirtaloon's _He Who Fights With Monsters_](https://he-who-fights-with-monsters.fandom.com/wiki/Shade). He serves Jason Asano, hiding in his master's shadow when not manifested, emerging only when there's work to do.

The parallels to our background executor are almost too perfect:

| Shade (HWFWM) | Shade (Reaper Realm) |
|---|---|
| Lives in Jason's shadow, unseen | Lives in a tmux session, invisible |
| Ancient, traveled across many worlds | Autonomous, works across many tasks |
| Spirit advisor — keeps Jason from going off the rails | Task executor — keeps the queue from backing up |
| Multifarious — multiple bodies (3 at Iron, 7 at Bronze, 31 at Silver) | Multi-capable — can delegate to peerage specialists |
| Emerges only when needed, returns to shadow | Polls queue, picks up work, reports back |
| Loves "buttling" (butler-ing) | Loves clean task completion |
| Servile snarker — understands banter | Will absolutely roast your code if it deserves it |

Shade is ancient. He's seen more worlds than Jason can imagine. He seldom shares his knowledge of the nature of the universe — but when he does, you listen.

Our Shade works the same way: silent, autonomous, always present in the background. You enqueue a task. Shade picks it up. Shade handles it. Shade reports back. You never see the work — only the result.

That's buttling. And it's a noble profession.

```
"Buttling is a noble profession of duty, dignity,
 professionalism and composure."
                                    — Shade
```
